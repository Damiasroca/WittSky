Placement

Drag a block on the frame. It is a 4:3 still, the same shape as 1600×1200. X and Y are thousandths of the frame from the anchor. Negative X moves left, negative Y moves up. The direction arrow rides the compass degree ring and the sample points south. Its colour runs from blue at 0 km/h through green, yellow and orange to red at 70 km/h. With the compass hidden, that mark is the centre of the circle it travels. The rain mark is only burned in while the station reports rain.
